I’m Anirudha Karande, a graduate researcher in robot learning at WPI.

I study how robots can learn physical skills from a few demonstrations, improve through safe practice, and keep learning over a lifetime, ultimately going beyond human capabilities.

Central question: How can a robot keep learning from its own practice, safely, for a lifetime?

My work spans imitation learning, safe reinforcement learning and continual learning. I am an M.S. student in Robotics Engineering at WPI, advised by Prof. Jing Xiao, and I am applying to PhD programs in robot learning.
