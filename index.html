I’m Anirudha Karande, a graduate researcher in robot learning at WPI.

I am an M.S. student in Robotics Engineering at WPI, advised by Prof. Jing Xiao.

My overarching research interest is to build robots that learn physical skills the way people do, from a few demonstrations, through safe practice, and over a lifetime, and then go beyond human limits. At the center of my work is learning adaptive compliance for physical interaction: knowing not only where to move, but how firmly to hold. It encompasses problems in imitation learning, reinforcement learning, optimization and control, and draws inspiration from computational sensorimotor learning and neuroscience.

I am applying to PhD programs in robot learning.
