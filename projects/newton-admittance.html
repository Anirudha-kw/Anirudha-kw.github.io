A Task-Space Admittance Controller for Newton

[Open source]

I contributed a task-space admittance controller with variable gains to Newton, the open-source GPU physics engine. It lets a position-controlled robot behave like a spring and damper around its target, so it gives way when it touches something and holds a chosen force. The gains can change at every step, for example from a learned policy, and the integration stays stable however they change.

The pull request: newton-physics/newton #4560.
