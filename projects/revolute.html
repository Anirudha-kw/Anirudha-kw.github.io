Revolute: Boston’s First Physical AI Hackathon

[3rd place · Aug 2026]

Our team placed 3rd at Revolute, Boston’s first Physical AI hackathon. In two days we built an imitation-learning pipeline for a Seeed B601 robot arm on LeRobot: teleoperated demonstrations with a leader arm, flow-matching policies, and models small enough to run on a Jetson Orin Nano. The tasks were kitchen chores: putting a slice of bread into a toaster, and moving toast from the toaster to a pan. Sourav Selvaraj’s write-up has the technical details.
